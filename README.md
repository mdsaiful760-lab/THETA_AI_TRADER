# THETA AI TRADER

## Mission

THETA AI TRADER is a professional AI-powered options trading platform designed for disciplined, explainable, and risk-aware trading.

The objective is not simply to generate signals, but to assist traders in making high-quality decisions using market structure, option Greeks, liquidity analysis, volatility analysis, and advanced risk management.
